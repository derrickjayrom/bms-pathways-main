# BMS Pathways

Build a modern, responsive multi-page web platform for Beyond Medical School (BMS) — "Mentorship. Exposure. Opportunity."

Brand & Design System:

- Palette: Sophisticated dark navy/blue (#0A192F / #1E293B / #0F172A), crisp white/light backgrounds, and refined gold/warm-amber accents (#D4AF37 / #E5A93C).
- Tone: Inspiring, professional, medical/academic without clinical/hospital sterility, youthful yet trustworthy. Modern sans-serif typography, rounded cards, subtle shadows and animations.

Pages & Core Features (React Router):

1. Home: Hero with headline ("Beyond Medical School. Beyond the Degree."), core tagline, CTAs ("Join the BMS Community", "Explore Our Programs"), "Why BMS?" feature cards (Mentorship, Exposure, Opportunity), "What We Do" 6 program cards, Vision & Mission, "Who Is BMS For?", Upcoming Events preview ("Coming Soon"), and prominent community CTA.
2. About BMS: Story, Mission, Vision, and 6 core value cards (Mentorship, Continuous Learning, Community, Excellence, Opportunity, Impact).
3. Programs: Categorized overview with tabs or sections for Career Development, Career Exploration, Mentorship, Exposure, and Research.
4. Mentorship: Value proposition for both Students and Mentors, feature highlights, and clear CTAs ("Find a Mentor", "Become a Mentor").
5. Events: Filterable event catalog (All, Seminars, Workshops, Mentorship, Career Development, Research) with sample upcoming events marked "Coming Soon" and registration modals.
6. Resources: Resource library with categorized cards (Career Guides, CV & Interview, Research, Postgraduate, Scholarships, Exams) and search/category filters.
7. Our Team: Leadership & team grid with placeholders for 8 key roles (Executive Lead, Programs, Mentorship, Research, Comms, Partnerships, Events, Tech) with bios and social links.
8. Join BMS: Multi-pathway registration with tabbed interactive forms for Students, Mentors/Speakers, and Partners, complete with validation and clean feedback states.
9. Contact: Contact form, inquiry details, FAQ/info, and placeholder social channels.

Include a sticky header with responsive mobile drawer navigation and an informative footer with newsletter signup and copyright.
